OUR MISSION

About PlateShare

Imagine a restaurant with suitable surplus food and a nearby shelter searching for meals. PlateShare aims to connect them through one convenient platform.

🌱 Our Vision

To reduce avoidable food waste by making responsible food donation easier to organise.

❤️ Our Mission

To connect restaurants with verified NGOs and shelters, coordinate pickups, and encourage transparent donations with food safety as a priority.

♻️ Our Values

Community support, accountability, responsible sharing, and sustainability.
